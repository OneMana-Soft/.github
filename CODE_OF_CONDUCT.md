# Code of conduct

Everyone who takes part in OneMana's projects, in issues, discussions, pull
requests or anywhere else we gather, agrees to the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, criticise ideas rather than people, and
leave room for those who are new.

## Reporting

Write to **support@onemana.dev** with "Conduct" in the subject. Reports are read
by the maintainer, kept private, and answered within 3 working days. Anyone who
breaks this code may be warned, have their comments removed, or be banned from
the projects, depending on what happened.
